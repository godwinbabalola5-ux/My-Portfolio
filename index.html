About Me

Hello, I'm Godwin Samuel Babalola, a software engineer from Lagos, Nigeria who loves turning ideas into things people can actually click, tap and enjoy using.

I'm an undergraduate at Lagos State University, soon to be a finalist, studying Computer Science Education. That mix of engineering and education shapes how I work: I build carefully, I explain clearly, and I always think about the person on the other side of the screen.

My work covers the whole stack. On the front end I create responsive, smooth interfaces with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React. On the back end I use Node.js, Express and MySQL to build REST APIs, secure sign-in systems with JWT and Bcrypt, and database-driven apps like wallet and transaction platforms.

I care just as much about how a product looks as how it runs. That is why I also design marketing flyers and branding materials in CorelDRAW and build responsive email campaigns for Mailchimp and Klaviyo.

I've built portfolios for other people, romantic and birthday websites, an admin dashboard and a VTU airtime platform. Right now I'm especially curious about cybersecurity and scalable digital solutions, and I'm always learning something new. If you have an idea, a team that needs another builder, or a brand that needs to stand out online, let's talk.
